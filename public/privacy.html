What we store

  • Your alias and a securely hashed password (we cannot read your password).
  • Your game stats, rating, and logs of finished games.
  • A login token kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

What we do not do

  • No email address is collected. No ads and no tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Hosting

The game runs on a third-party host (currently Render), which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses and request times.

Your control

Use Delete account on the home screen to permanently remove your account, rating and stats. Your name is replaced by "deleted" in old match logs.
